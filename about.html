About Maple Charm LLC

Maple Charm LLC is a U.S.-registered company specializing in premium scented candles designed to bring warmth, comfort, and elegance into everyday living spaces.

Our company operates under the legal name Maple Charm LLC, with a registered business address at 1001 S Main St Ste 600, Kalispell, MT 59901-1498, United States.

At Maple Charm, we focus on creating high-quality candles that combine aesthetic design with carefully crafted fragrances. Each product is thoughtfully developed to enhance atmosphere, whether for relaxation, home décor, or meaningful moments. Our candles are made using selected materials and refined scent blends to ensure a clean burn and a pleasant, long-lasting aroma.

We believe that a simple product like a candle can transform a space and elevate everyday experiences. That is why we are committed to delivering products that reflect quality, simplicity, and a sense of comfort.

Our Quality Commitment

Every candle we create reflects our dedication to excellence and attention to detail.
