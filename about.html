About MAPLE CHARM LLC

Discover our story and passion for creating exceptional scented candles

Our Story

Founded in 2020, MAPLE CHARM LLC began with a simple mission: to create premium scented candles that transform spaces and elevate everyday moments into extraordinary experiences.

What started as a small home-based operation has grown into a beloved brand, serving customers across the country who appreciate quality craftsmanship and exceptional fragrances.

Our commitment to excellence drives everything we do, from sourcing the finest ingredients to perfecting our unique scent combinations.

Our Craftsmanship

Every candle we create is a testament to our dedication to quality and artistry.

  • Premium Ingredients: We use only the finest wax blends and essential oils
  • Handcrafted Excellence: Each candle is carefully poured and finished by hand
  • Sustainable Practices: We prioritize eco-friendly materials and processes
  • Quality Testing: Every batch undergoes rigorous quality control
